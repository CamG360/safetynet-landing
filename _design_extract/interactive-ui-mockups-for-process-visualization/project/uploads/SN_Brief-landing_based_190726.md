## Section 9 — FAQ

### Privacy & Security

**How does SafetyNet protect my privacy?**
Only stores check-in times, emergency contacts, custom messages. No continuous location tracking, no background data collection. Safety information only shared with chosen contacts if an alert triggers.

**What security and reliability measures will SafetyNet have?**
Industry-standard encryption, reliable cloud infrastructure with backup systems, multi-channel alert delivery.

**Who can see the essential information I include?**
No one when you set up. Only when an alert is triggered do chosen contacts get access.

**What do you do with my data?**
Nothing. We don't sell your data. Ever.

---

### How It Works

**How does it work offline or with a dead battery?**
SafetyNet doesn't rely on your phone to send the alert — servers send it. When you set a check-in time, servers start a countdown. If you check in safe, countdown stops. If you don't (dead battery, no signal, can't reach phone) — alert sends automatically. Phone only needs to work at setup and check-in.

**Will this drain my battery?**
No. Unlike location sharing apps, SafetyNet does not track your GPS in the background. Monitoring timer runs entirely on servers.

**What if I forget to check in but I'm safe?**
Customizable extra time and reminder notifications before any alert is sent. If alert goes out, cancel with one tap.

**What are SafetyNet's limitations?**
- Requires initial internet connection to set check-in time
- Relies on emergency contact's ability to receive SMS or email
- Cannot prevent emergencies — only facilitates faster response
- No system is 100% foolproof — always have backup safety plans

SafetyNet is an additional layer of protection, not a replacement for common sense safety practices.

---

### Why SafetyNet

**How is this different from Find My Friends or Life360?**
Location sharing apps require your phone to be working. If you can't reach it, they fail silently — contacts see your last location and don't know if you're okay or in trouble. SafetyNet sends a clear alert with context and instructions. No ambiguity.

**Will my contacts get constant notifications?**
No. Contacts only hear from SafetyNet when a Safety Alert is triggered.

---

### Getting Started

**What does my emergency contact need to do?**
One-time confirmation message at setup — takes 10 seconds, no app required. After that, they hear nothing unless an alert triggers. If alert fires: SMS, email, and push notification (if they have the app) with custom message and instructions.

**Does my emergency contact need the app?**
No. Alerts via SMS and email. App optional for push notifications.

**What devices does SafetyNet work on?**
iOS, Android, and web browsers.

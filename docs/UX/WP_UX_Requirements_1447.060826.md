Brand Strategist Requirements (The Brand Identity)
Color Palette:

Primary/Action: Rich teal/emerald green (used heavily for the "Get Early Access" buttons, primary navigation, and "Active" status indicators).

Backgrounds: A moody, dark twilight blue for the hero section to establish a nighttime/vulnerable setting, transitioning to clean white and off-white for the information-heavy sections below.

System Status: Mint green for positive reinforcement (checkmarks, "Sent" receipts) and subtle amber/orange for warnings ("No tap detected").

Typography Guidelines:

A clean, modern, high-legibility sans-serif font family.

High-contrast, heavily weighted headlines (e.g., "Text me when you're safe fails when you can't text.") to create immediate emotional impact.

Imagery & Photography Style:

Real-world Photography: Relatable, everyday scenarios highlighting vulnerability (e.g., a person walking alone at dusk).

Visual Metaphors: The glowing blue/teal "protection sphere" overlaid on photographs to communicate a digital safety shield without relying on aggressive or scary imagery.

Minimalist Graphics: Simple line-art illustrations used to contrast scenarios (e.g., the isolated person vs. the connected network).

Brand Voice (Visual Translation): Calm, reassuring, and proactive. The visual tone emphasizes privacy ("Zero tracking") and peace of mind rather than fear-mongering.

UI/UX Designer Requirements (The Interface & Experience)
Grid Systems & Layout:

A centered, single-column alignment in the hero section that acts as a funnel, drawing the user's eye from the headline, down to the primary CTA, and finally to the app interface mockup.

Modular, card-based grid structures for chunking out Use Cases (e.g., Daily Travel, Modern Dating) and Testimonials.

Component Libraries (UI Kits):

CTAs: Rounded, solid-fill primary buttons, often paired with a directional arrow (Get Early Access ->) to encourage forward momentum.

Floating Mockups: Layered, drop-shadowed cards that simulate the actual app interface (e.g., the timeline showing the check-in countdown), allowing users to "see" the product immediately.

Trust Badges: Pill-shaped tags with checkmarks for quick feature scanning (e.g., ✓ Automatic alerts, ✓ Zero tracking).

Comparison Matrix: A styled table component that directly compares SafetyNet's feature set against "Standard Location Apps" using clear "Included" vs. "Not included" visuals.

Interactive States & Architecture:

A categorized, filterable FAQ section using expanding accordions and pill-shaped tab filters (All, Privacy & Security, Launch & Pricing) alongside a dedicated search input.

A persistent, sticky top navigation bar ensuring the primary conversion button is always accessible as the user scrolls.

Spacing & Rhythm:

Generous whitespace and distinct color-blocking to clearly separate the emotional hook (the hero section) from the logical arguments (How it Works, Comparison Table, and FAQs).
